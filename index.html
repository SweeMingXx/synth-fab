Make yourself at home.

Scroll to move through the fabrication story. Use the five stage buttons to jump directly to a handoff.

Desktop: drag the model to orbit. Click ⤢ for inspection mode, then wheel to zoom. Press Esc to return to page scrolling.

Touch: tap ⤢ first to orbit or pinch to zoom; tap it again to resume scrolling. In concept stages, orbit reveals the 3D interpretation.

Keyboard: focus the canvas, press Enter to inspect, then use ← → ↑ ↓ to orbit, +/− to zoom, and R to reset.

The AMS palette changes the preview only. “Explode” reveals a gyroid-inspired core. The layer slider samples real cross-sections of the procedural mesh; travel moves and infill are illustrative.

Honest by design: no Gemini, ChatGPT, Hunyuan3D, or Bambu API is connected. This is an independent portfolio concept, not an affiliated product or validated manufacturing workflow.
